Your address book, with a memory.

BetterContacts keeps your relationships alive instead of just storing names:

- Search like you think: ask "who did I meet this week?" and get an answer, not a keyword match
- Smart reminders tell you when a close friendship is going quiet
- Never miss a birthday again
- Tag where you met people, and find everyone in Berlin in one tap
- Keep a close circle, and give it the attention it deserves

Everything runs on your device. Your contacts never leave your phone — no accounts, no tracking, no cloud.

Filtering, sorting, and touchpoints that keep your relationships warm. That's it. That's the app.
